LIVE | Yang di-Pertuan Agong Sultan Ibrahim Sultan Iskandar has granted Najib Abdul Razak house arrest until Aug 23, 2028.

However, the arrangement is conditional on the jailed former prime minister paying a RM50 million fine.

*Malaysiakini *brings you updates live.

**Anwar's legacy at stake **

**5pm - **The government must use the remainder of its current term to implement institutional reforms it previously promised during election periods, says Muda. 

In light of Najib’s conditional pardon, Muda points out in a statement that Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim’s legacy stands to be that of a “kleptocrat saviour” if he fails to implement reforms advocated by his administration.

**4.45pm -** DAP's central executive council is calling for an emergency meeting tomorrow to discuss the party's next course of action following the clemency granted to Najib Abdul Razak.

In a statement, DAP secretary-general Anthony Loke says the party maintains its position on the 1MDB scandal, demanding justice and accountability for everyone involved in plundering the state investment arm.

According to him, the party's stand was conveyed by Minister in the Prime Minister's Department (Federal Territories) Hannah Yeoh to the Pardons Board in her capacity as a board member.

![](https://s.malaysiakini.com/i/f5L2qNoP4ZJrFfYlGDnUXwWdaKo=/200x0/smart/filters:strip_exif()/file/publisher-c1a3f893382d2b2f8a9aa22a654d9c97/2026/08/c3adb580d86b2e3903bf8495d7ca4d6f.jpg)

"As she has explained, the views were conveyed honestly and frankly in the interest of the country.

"At the same time, DAP respects the Yang di-Pertuan Agong’s power of pardon as provided for under the Federal Constitution.

"DAP has always upheld the principles of constitutional monarchy and constitutional supremacy, while defending the rule of law and the fight against corruption," Loke says. 

**'Leaked' document examined**

**3.40pm -** An examination of the “leaked” pardon document circulating on social media last night has found several key differences from what was granted to Najib Abdul Razak this morning.

The document, which Minister in the Prime Minister’s Department (Federal Territories) Hannah Yeoh has denied signing and described as fake, purportedly allows the former prime minister to serve his sentence under house arrest on the condition that he pays all outstanding fines.

However, the house arrest granted to Najib today was on the condition that he pays a RM50 million fine. Attorney-General Dusuki Mokhtar has clarified that the clemency did not extend to Najib's 1MDB conviction, in which he was fined RM11.38 billion.

The "leaked" document further states that failure to pay the fines would result in an additional year being added to his prison term. It also does not specify whether the pardon applies only to Najib’s current sentence or extends to any future sentences. Nor does it state that he will complete his sentence on Aug 23, 2028.

It also claims that the Yang di-Pertuan Agong was advised to allow Najib to continue serving his prison sentence, but that the King decided to grant the former premier clemency instead.

*Malaysiakini *has contacted Dusuki and Yeoh, who both sit on the Federal Territories Pardons Board, for clarification.

**PAS welcomes pardon**

**3.30pm -** PAS is describing Najib Abdul Razak’s conditional pardon as deeply meaningful to Malaysians who appreciate the former prime minister’s contributions and service in developing the nation for the people’s prosperity.

In a statement, PAS secretary-general Takiyuddin Hassan says the Islamist party will continue praying for Najib’s health while he serves the remainder of his sentence under house arrest.

The Kota Bharu MP says his party is also confident that Najib could continue contributing his ideas, views, and experience for the good of the nation.

Meanwhile, PAS information chief Ahmad Fadhli Shaari is reminding DAP deputy chairperson Nga Kor Ming of his [pledge to resign](https://www.malaysiakini.com/news/779530) from the cabinet if Najib is released without serving a punishment commensurate with his offence.

“Congratulations Najib. Now is the time for (Nga) to fulfil his promise to resign. If possible, take DAP out of the government as well,” says the Pasir Mas MP on Facebook.

Speaking to reporters in Putrajaya, he says the clemency does not mean the country is bowing to corruption or thievery.

"I want to stress that all cases, including those involving Najib that are currently ongoing (in court), must proceed according to due process. The same applies to other cases involving corruption.

"This country will no longer take lightly any misconduct or abuse of power to enrich any leader or any member of the public. This is our position," he says.

**Agong's discretion**

**2.45pm -** Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim says allowing Najib Abdul Razak to serve the remainder of his jail sentence under house arrest is the sole discretion of the Yang di-Pertuan Agong.

He says that, as prime minister, his duty was to advise the ruler about legal implications, national stability, and the rule of law, as well as to take into account the guidance of the attorney-general.

"But ultimately, the Constitution provides for the absolute power of the Yang di-Pertuan Agong," he tells reporters after performing Friday prayers in Putrajaya.

**House arrest until 2028**

**12.40pm - **Yang di-Pertuan Agong Sultan Ibrahim Sultan Iskandar has granted Najib Abdul Razak house arrest until Aug 23, 2028.

However, the arrangement is conditional on the jailed former prime minister paying a RM50 million fine.

The date coincides with the end of Najib’s reduced prison sentence for the SRC International case, while the RM50 million payment is the same amount as the fine imposed in that case.

Both the 2028 release date and RM50 million fine resulted from the previous Agong, Sultan Abdullah Sultan Ahmad Shah’s [decision to reduce](https://www.malaysiakini.com/news/695120) Najib’s sentence for the SRC case.

Separately, Najib was found guilty by the High Court in the 1MDB corruption case and sentenced to [15 years in prison](https://www.malaysiakini.com/news/764365) and an RM11.38 billion fine over the scandal, which the judge compared to the conquests of [Attila the Hun](https://www.malaysiakini.com/news/777588).

The 1MDB sentence is to begin after Najib completes his SRC sentence.

It remains unclear how today’s clemency decision will affect the 1MDB sentence. The case is currently under appeal.
