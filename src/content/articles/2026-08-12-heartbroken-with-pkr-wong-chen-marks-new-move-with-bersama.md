Former Subang MP Wong Chen has officially joined Bersama today after 17 years as a PKR member.

Speaking at a press conference at Bersama’s party headquarters in Puchong, Wong (*above, second from right*) described his decision to leave PKR and vacate the Subang parliamentary seat as “heartbreaking” but necessary under the anti-party hopping provisions.

“I am excited to join this party,” said Wong, who cited a list of “personal insults” to his career while in government and his professional background as a corporate lawyer as a reason for his departure.

Specifically, Wong alluded to his removal as the non-executive chairperson of Malaysia Debt Ventures (MDV) in May, despite leading the GLC under the Finance Ministry back to profitability during his three-year tenure.

“They removed me in five days. I took that as a personal insult. There was no explanation given,” he said.

![](https://i.newscdn.net/publisher-c1a3f893382d2b2f8a9aa22a654d9c97/2026/08/9a3463097811ccfd3f6168ceb9b15747.jpg)

“Two or three weeks later, Treasury secretary-general Johan Mahmood Merican wrote me a very nice letter thanking me for my service, but he conveniently stamped the word ‘*sulit*’ (confidential), so I cannot show it to you.

“To this day, I have not received a call from the prime minister (Anwar Ibrahim). It was his ultimate decision as the prime minister. The Finance Ministry owns MDV. So for me, as a corporate lawyer for 22 years, I took that very personally,” he added.

At the time, Wong’s removal from MDV followed a rapidly worsening relationship with Anwar’s administration.

![](https://i.ncdn.xyz/publisher-c1a3f893382d2b2f8a9aa22a654d9c97/2020/11/1b5816a9d0f4c612176411728b5792ef.jpg)

On May 17, Wong was among PKR MPs who attended Bersama’s launch by co-leaders Rafizi Ramli and Nik Nazmi Nik Ahmad, both of whom were present today to welcome Wong into the party.

He reportedly discovered on May 20 that his parliamentary office had been [blocked](https://www.malaysiakini.com/news/775818) from the MyKhas allocation portal, and publicly protested the move on May 22.

Three days later, the Finance Ministry issued the letter [terminating](https://www.malaysiakini.com/news/776155) his MDV chairpersonship, effective May 31.
